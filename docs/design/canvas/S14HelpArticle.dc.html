Payments & wallet
When do I get a refund to my wallet?

When a ride is cancelled after you already paid part of it from your wallet, the amount you paid goes straight back to your wallet. You'll get a notification when the refund lands.

If a driver cancels on the way to you, you never pay anything — any held wallet amount is released right away.

Refunds granted by our support team also arrive as wallet credit. You can see every refund in your wallet's activity list, marked "Refund".

Wallet credit never expires and is used automatically on your next ride before cash.
