- **SRS IDs with no row:** USR-02, CON-03, NFR-U1. The others (AUTH-07, ES-01/04/06/07, NFR-A2/P1/S1/S4/M1, CON-01/02/04–06, ASM-*, SD-*) cannot be observed live, or are covered implicitly (AUTH-07 = password login of the seeded accounts).

- **Cross-cutting device states with no row:** system dark mode, maximum font scale, rotation lock, the soft keyboard covering inputs, Android hardware Back on sheets during a ride, a throttled (slow) network, and app kill while an offer is open.
- **Multi-role and multi-region gaps:** self-dispatch for R2 (rider and driver on one account), a rider who is passenger on one ride while online as a driver, booking and SOS in a second region, and a driver outside the region of their service types.
